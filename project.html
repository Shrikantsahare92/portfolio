🏫 Kindergarden School Website

A web-based platform designed for a kindergarten school to improve communication between teachers, students, and parents. It provides a simple and effective way to share updates, activities, and important announcements.

The website focuses on a friendly, colorful, and easy-to-use interface suitable for young children while also meeting the needs of teachers and parents.

Key Features:

  • Teacher–parent communication for notices and updates
  • Student activity and homework sharing
  • Event and schedule information for parents
  • Child-friendly and engaging design
  • Fully responsive across devices
